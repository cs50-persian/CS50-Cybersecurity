## 📖 About This Project

This repository is part of the **CS50x Persian** initiative, created to make high-quality computer science and cybersecurity education accessible to **Persian-speaking learners**.

The project provides Persian-language resources for **Harvard University's CS50 Cybersecurity**, helping students learn the fundamentals of cybersecurity, including how to secure accounts, data, systems, and software while preserving privacy.

Through **CS50x Persian**, Persian-speaking students can access professionally translated educational content, course materials, exercises, and learning resources designed to make the CS50 Cybersecurity curriculum easier to follow and understand.

Our goal is simple:

**To make world-class computer science and cybersecurity education accessible to Persian speakers. ❤️**

---

## 🎓 About CS50 Cybersecurity

**CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity** is Harvard University's introductory course to cybersecurity.

The course introduces students to the fundamental principles of cybersecurity and explores how individuals and organizations can protect their digital information, accounts, systems, and software.

Throughout the course, students learn practical approaches to cybersecurity and develop an understanding of how to identify security risks, protect sensitive information, and preserve privacy in an increasingly connected world.

The Persian edition makes these concepts accessible to students who prefer learning cybersecurity and technical subjects in Persian.

---

## 📚 What You'll Learn

Throughout CS50 Cybersecurity, you will learn:

* Securing Data
* Securing Systems
* Securing Software
* Securing Accounts
* Preserving Privacy

By the end of the course, you will have a strong foundation in cybersecurity and a better understanding of how to protect accounts, data, systems, and software while maintaining privacy and security in everyday digital environments.
